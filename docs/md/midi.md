# MIDI

- **In (Keyboard mode)** — note on/off and pitch bend (±2 semitones).
- **Out** — the sequencer (and/or what the voice is playing) as MIDI notes, so you can record the pattern in the DAW or sequence another plugin. Accent = velocity 127, otherwise 100. The plugin is a synth with MIDI in and MIDI out.

VST3 and standalone. Audio Unit on macOS.
