# Effects

Click the page bar to **Effects**. The LFO lanes stay on the right. Everything is **off** until you enable it, which is a kindness.

Left rail: **On** enables that effect. Click the rest of the box to select it — settings fill the whole panel between the rail and the LFO column. A six-dot handle on the left of each box drags the chain order. Order is stored with the patch. Nothing selected: the yellow field, the logo, and **Click an effect to add it**.

The insert **Equalizer** is the same five-band parametric as Pre/Post, but it sits *in* the chain. Pre/Post sit around the whole chain and are edited on the EQ page. Each effect’s visualizer shows the post-FX spectrum plus that effect’s sketch.

### Chorus

Rate, Depth, Feedback, Mix, Centre (1–20 ms), Width.

### Compressor

Threshold (−60 to 0 dB), Ratio (1–20), Attack (0.1–100 ms), Release (10–500 ms), Mix, Makeup (−6 to +24 dB), Knee (0–12 dB), Highpass on the detector (20–400 Hz). The meter is gain reduction.

### Delay

**Sync** on: **Division** is 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, or 1/2 at tempo. **Sync** off: **Time** is free. Then Feedback, Lowpass, Mix, Ping (cross-channel), Highpass in the feedback, Spread.

### Distortion

Type Soft / Hard / Fold / Rectify, Drive, Bias, Tone, Mix. Separate from the synth **Drive** knob.

### Equalizer

Five bands plus Trim. Click a band on the plot, then set Type, Frequency, Gain, Q. Spectrum behind the curve is the post-FX FFT.

### Filter

Type Low / Band / High, Slope 12 / 24 dB, Cutoff (20–12000 Hz), Resonance, Drive, Mix. Extra filter in the FX chain, not the 303 ladder.

### Flanger

Rate, Depth, Feedback, Mix, Centre (0.5–8 ms), Width.

### Phaser

Rate, Depth, Feedback, Centre (200–4000 Hz), Stages 2 / 4 / 6 / 8, Mix.

### Reverb

Size, Damping, Width, Mix, Predelay (0–200 ms), Freeze, Low Cut.
