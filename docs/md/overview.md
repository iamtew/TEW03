# Hello, acid

TEW03 is a one-voice acid synth. Classic 303 squelch, plus a bit of extra nastiness for when polite is not the brief.

The step sequencer is how you play it most of the time. Patterns drive the voice, and they also leave as MIDI, so you can record the line in a DAW or point it at another plugin.

This is the user book. Internals live under Spec, Architecture, and DSP notes in the sidebar, if you enjoy reading the wiring.

VST3 and standalone, with Audio Unit on macOS.
