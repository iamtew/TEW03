TEW03

Everybody Needs a 303

— Fatboy Slim

You wanted a 303 that still bites, and a sequencer that actually lives in the plugin. TEW03 is a one-voice acid synth: saw or square, a resonant diode ladder, accent, slide, drive, and a Nasty knob for extra grit after the filter.

It is part of Stupid Audio — six free instruments. The rest of the pack lives over there.

GitHub

VST3, Audio Unit on macOS, and standalone. Windows, Linux, and Mac.

Main

This is the instrument: the voice, a 3×12 step sequencer, and four drawn LFOs you can drop on any knob. Sixteen steps, or thirty-two if you hit 2x. Accent and slide per step. Key lock if you want it in scale. Patterns play the voice and go out as MIDI, so you can record the line in the DAW or drive something else.

LFO 1 LFO 2 LFO 3 LFO 4

  • Keyboard — play it from MIDI. The sequencer can still clock for MIDI out.
  • Standalone — loop the selected slot. Hidden from the Play list in a DAW.
  • Pattern — hold a key to trigger a pattern.

Effects

Chorus, compressor, delay, distortion, EQ, filter, flanger, phaser, reverb. Click to add one, drag to reorder the chain. Everything starts off, so you are not fighting a surprise wash of reverb on a dry acid line.

EQ

Five-band parametric before the effects and another five after. The spectrum sits behind the curve so you can see what is leaving the chain while you nudge a band.

Download TEW03

The Windows installer is the easy path. It can put the VST3, the standalone app, or both on the machine, and it adds a Start menu shortcut for the app.

Windows installer

The VST3 zip is just the plugin. Drop it where your DAW looks for VST3s. Mac builds are ad-hoc signed, so Gatekeeper will sulk until you right-click Open the first time.

Windows Linux macOS

The standalone zip is the app, no DAW required. Same Gatekeeper story on Mac. Linux wants the usual system ALSA, FreeType, fontconfig, and X11 libraries.
